## Install — Android (Chrome)
1. Open the URL in Chrome (not Samsung Internet or a WhatsApp/in-app browser).
2. ⋮ (top right) → "Install app" (older phones: "Add to Home screen" → Install).
3. Open from the home screen once while online. Allow Location and Camera.
4. Offline from then on.

## Install — iPhone (Safari)
Share → Add to Home Screen. Open once online.

## Phones that already have the apps
Open each app once while online after the upload — it updates itself.
